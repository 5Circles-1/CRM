# Caller — how to use the CRM

> **The canonical training now lives inside the CRM**, under the **Training**
> tab: role-aware modules, a searchable UI glossary, a per-module check and a
> recorded acknowledgement, with the live configuration substituted into the
> text. Source: `api/content/training/`. This file remains for classroom and
> offline use — if the two ever disagree, the in-app module is correct.

You are the first voice the client hears. Your job in the CRM is small and
strict: **every lead you touch leaves with a next step on it.** Everything else
in this document exists to make that easy.

---

## Your day, in order

1. **Press "Start shift"** (top right). Until you do, the system will not give
   you leads. It is not being awkward — a lead sitting with someone who is not
   at their desk is a lead nobody is calling.
2. Open **My Day**. Work it top to bottom. It is already in the right order:
   immediate first, then whatever is most overdue, then the rest.
3. **Press "End shift"** when you leave. Your hours come from this, and so does
   the 9-hour figure your admin sees.

You should never have to decide what to call next. If you find yourself
choosing, tell your counsellor — that means the ordering is wrong.

---

## Logging a call

Press **Log a call** on the lead. Three things matter.

**Talk time** goes in as `mm:ss` — `3:07` for three minutes seven seconds. You
can still type plain seconds if you prefer. Be honest here: a call under 30
seconds does not count as a connect no matter which outcome you choose, and
your connect rate is built from this number.

**The outcome** must be what actually happened. The full list:

| Outcome | Use it when | What the CRM does |
|---|---|---|
| Connected — interested | They want to know more | You must set a follow-up time |
| Connected — asked to call back | They named a time | You must set the callback time |
| Will visit the office | They said they are coming in | Schedules a check the next day |
| Will call us back themselves | They will ring us | Waits **2 days** before chasing |
| Disconnected after introduction | They hung up during your opening | Waits **1 day** |
| Connected — not interested | A clear no | **Closes the lead** |
| Not answered | Rang out | Retries in 3 hours |
| Incoming unavailable | Could not be reached at all | Retries in 4 hours |
| Busy | Engaged | Retries in 1 hour |
| Switched off | Phone off | Retries in 4 hours |
| Wrong person | Not the person we wanted | Stays open |
| Language barrier | Cannot converse | Stays open — ask for a transfer |
| Job enquiry — not a client | Asking about a job with us | **Closes the lead** as invalid |
| Invalid number | Not a real number | **Closes the lead** |
| Do not call | They asked us to stop | **Closes the lead** |

**A next step.** If the outcome is not one of the four closing ones, the lead
keeps a next action. Usually the CRM schedules it for you. For *interested* and
*asked to call back* you must set the time yourself, because only you know what
was agreed.

### Two you will get wrong at first

- **Job enquiry** is not "not interested". Someone asking about a job was never
  a client. Marking them not-interested makes it look like we lost a sale we
  never had, and quietly damages everybody's conversion rate.
- **Will call us back themselves** is not "callback requested". Callback
  requested means *we* ring *them* at a set time. Will-call-back means they
  ring us — so the CRM deliberately waits two days instead of chasing them
  tomorrow, which would contradict what you agreed.

## The ten-minute rule

A new lead is yours for **ten minutes**. If you have not called it in that time
it moves to the next caller on your team, then the next, and so on. If nobody
has touched it after everyone has had a turn, **it comes back to you** and it
is yours to finish.

This is not a punishment and nobody is told off for it. It exists because a
lead sitting untouched is worth nothing. When one leaves you it disappears from
your list entirely — that is normal.

**You cannot pass a lead you have already started.** Once you have logged a
single call it is yours until a counsellor moves it. If you genuinely cannot
work it — wrong language, abusive, personal conflict — ask your counsellor.

## Alerts and reminders

The **🔔 bell** at the top shows everything waiting on you. Red means something
is already late.

Callbacks pop up on their own, bottom-right, when they fall due. They appear
**once**. If you dismiss one, it stays in the bell — it is not lost.

The page refreshes itself every 30 seconds. You do **not** need to press F5 to
see new leads.

If the popups are too frequent, say so — the interval and which alerts pop are
settings your admin can change in a minute. Do not just start ignoring them.

---

## Re-tapping: finding people you already spoke to

**Find lead** has one-click lists across the top:

- **Not answered** — everyone whose last call rang out
- **Asked for a callback** — everyone waiting on you to ring back
- **Busy** / **Switched off** — worth another go at a different time of day
- **Overdue now** — anything past its promised time
- **Never contacted** — leads with no call at all against them

The filter looks at your **most recent** call. Someone who missed once and then
picked up drops off the not-answered list automatically.

---

## WhatsApp

**Mark WhatsApp sent** on the lead when you message them. The CRM cannot see
WhatsApp, so this is your word for it. It matters because "has anyone messaged
them?" gets asked constantly, and the alternative is guessing.

Untick it if you pressed it by mistake.

---

## Walk-ins

If they say they are coming in, log **Will visit the office**. When they
actually arrive, press **Mark walked in** on their lead.

These are two different numbers and only one of them becomes revenue. Do not
mark a walk-in for someone who only promised.

---

## Your numbers

**Performance** shows your dials, connects, interested, not-interested,
callbacks, visits promised, walk-ins, WhatsApp messages and talk time.

**My Score** is scored for self-reflection, not for punishment. Components you
had nothing to do are excluded from both your points *and* the total — you are
never marked down for a thing that did not come up.

---

## 🟢 Green flags — you are doing this right

- Your My Day list empties by end of shift
- Your bell is empty or close to it
- Talk time is going up while dial count stays steady — you are having real
  conversations, not just ringing phones
- Callbacks are logged at the time the client actually named
- Leads leave you because you closed them, not because ten minutes ran out

## 🔴 Red flags — fix these today

- **Leads leaving you on the ten-minute rule.** You are not starting your list.
- **Every call logged as "not answered".** Sometimes true. Every day, not true.
  This is the easiest thing in the CRM to spot from the outside.
- **Connect rate high but talk time tiny.** Calls under 30 seconds are not
  connects. Logging them as connected does not make them count and does make
  the number look dishonest.
- **A bell that is always red.** Something is being ignored.
- **Marking leads "not interested" to clear your list.** This closes them
  permanently and it is visible.
- **Marking a walk-in that never happened.** This one is checkable at the door.

## Problems you will hit

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A lead vanished from My Day | Ten minutes passed, or a counsellor moved it | Normal. Find it under **Find lead** if you need it |
| "You cannot close a call without a next action" | The outcome you chose needs a follow-up time | Set the time, or pick a closing outcome if it is genuinely over |
| No new leads arriving | You are off shift, or none have come in | Check the shift button says **On floor** |
| A lead you never called is back with you | It went round everyone untouched | It is yours now. Call it |
| Talk time will not save | It wants `mm:ss` | `3:07`, not `3.07` |
