# Counsellor — how to use the CRM

You are the team lead and the closer. Two jobs, and the CRM treats them
differently: **the floor is yours to supervise, and the money is yours to
collect.**

Read the caller guide too. You cannot supervise a screen you have not used.

---

## Your day, in order

1. **Start shift.** Your hours count the same as anyone's.
2. Open **Floor**. Look at *Pipeline leaks* first — that number should be
   heading toward zero, not sitting still.
3. Work your qualified leads and your callbacks.
4. Open **Collections** once in the morning and once before you leave.
5. Open **Performance** at the end of the day. Two minutes, every day.

---

## What only you can do

**Transfer a lead.** Callers cannot move their own leads, deliberately: if they
could, the difficult ones would circulate forever and nobody would own
anything. When a caller asks for a transfer they are asking *you*.

Good reasons: language mismatch, four unanswered attempts, a caller off sick
with a full list, a personal conflict. Bad reason: "they don't like it." A lead
is capped at **2 manual transfers**, after which it goes to nurture — that cap
exists so a hard lead stops being passed around instead of worked.

**Book a deal**, record payments, and chase instalments. The counsellor who
closes the deal chases the money. That does not move when the client is handed
to the advisory team.

**Qualify into your pipeline.** A caller sends you a lead; from there it is
yours.

---

## The ten-minute rule, and what it tells you

New leads move between callers automatically if untouched for ten minutes,
round the team, then back to whoever had them first.

**You do not need to do anything about this.** But watch it, because it is the
single most useful signal on the floor:

- One caller constantly losing leads → they are not starting their list. Talk
  to them today, not at the end of the month.
- Leads going all the way round and coming home → nobody is picking anything
  up. That is a floor problem, not a person problem.
- Almost no automatic moves → healthy. This is what it should look like.

The rule only ever fires on leads with **no call logged at all**. A lead that
has been worked never moves on its own.

---

## Watching your callers

**Performance** shows everyone on your team: dials, connects, interested,
not-interested, no-answer, callbacks, visits promised, walk-ins, WhatsApp
messages, talk time, deals, revenue, and the three rates. Sort by any column —
click **Walked in** to see who is actually bringing people through the door.

You see your team. You do not see the other team, and they do not see yours.

A dash in a percentage column means there was nothing to divide by. Someone who
made no calls has no conversion rate; showing 0% would rank them beside a caller
who worked forty leads and closed none.

### Reading it honestly

- **Dials alone mean nothing.** Forty dials and nine minutes of talk time is
  someone ringing phones, not selling.
- **Connect rate needs talk time beside it.** A connect requires 30+ seconds of
  actual conversation, so a high connect rate with low talk time is arithmetic
  that does not add up.
- **Interested-to-deal is your number, not theirs.** If callers are producing
  interest and it is not converting, look at yourself before you look at them.
- **Compare like with like.** A caller on immediate leads and one on aged
  leads are not doing the same job.

---

## Collections

Money that has been promised and not arrived is the cheapest revenue in the
business — it is already sold.

Check **Collections** twice a day. Overdue instalments are yours to chase even
after the client has been handed to the advisory team. The thermometer shows
where the month stands against the ₹7,00,000 breakeven.

---

## 🟢 Green flags

- Pipeline leaks trending down through the day
- Few automatic reassignments — your callers are starting their lists
- Talk time rising across the team
- Walk-ins converting at a visibly better rate than phone-only leads
- Your overdue instalment list is short and getting shorter
- Callbacks being honoured at the time the client named

## 🔴 Red flags

- **Pipeline leaks flat or rising.** Nothing is being worked to completion.
- **The same caller losing leads to the ten-minute rule every day.** Coach them
  now. In a month it will be a performance conversation instead.
- **Transfers being requested for the same lead repeatedly.** Nobody wants it.
  Decide: work it yourself, or nurture it. Do not keep it moving.
- **A caller with a high connect rate and low talk time.** Look at the calls.
- **"Not interested" spiking.** It closes leads permanently. Sometimes it is
  honest; a sudden rise usually is not.
- **Job enquiries logged as "not interested".** Wrong outcome, and it makes
  your team's conversion look worse than it is. Correct it when you see it.
- **Overdue instalments ageing past two weeks.** Every week costs you.
- **Walk-ins marked but no deals following.** Either they are not really coming
  in, or something is going wrong at the meeting.

## Problems you will hit

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Only a counsellor or admin may transfer a lead" | A caller tried to transfer | Correct — they ask you |
| A transfer is refused | The lead has hit its cap of 2 | Work it yourself or send it to nurture |
| A caller cannot see a lead they had | It moved, automatically or by you | Expected. It is with its new owner |
| A lead is back with its first caller untouched | It went round everyone | Ask why nobody started it |
| Collections shows an instalment you have been paid | The payment is not recorded | Record it. The CRM cannot see your bank |
| Your team's numbers look wrong | Usually a wrong outcome logged | Open the lead and read its timeline |
